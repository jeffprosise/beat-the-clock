# Common e-mail signals

These are heuristics, not proof. Use the whole message and surrounding correspondence.

- **Booking/confirmation:** confirmation, confirmed, record locator, reservation number, ticket number, booking reference, itinerary receipt.
- **Change:** schedule change, updated itinerary, modification confirmed, new departure time, room/date changed.
- **Cancellation:** canceled/cancelled, refund initiated, reservation canceled, flight canceled.
- **Transportation:** airline and flight number, airport codes, departure/arrival times, train number, transfer/pickup details.
- **Lodging:** property name, check-in/check-out dates, address, confirmation number, room type.
- **Activity:** ticket/voucher, meeting point, start time, tour operator, admission date, confirmation number.
- **Likely marketing:** sale, save %, earn bonus miles, recommended for your trip, upgrade offer, survey, newsletter.

A later timestamp alone does not prove supersession. Look for a shared confirmation number, record locator, booking ID, flight number/date, hotel/date range, or explicit language tying the newer message to the older one.
