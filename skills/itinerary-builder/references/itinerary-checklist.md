# Itinerary completeness checklist

Before calling an itinerary complete, check for:

- Every flight/train leg, including connection points
- Arrival dates that differ from departure dates on overnight flights
- Hotel booked for an early-morning arrival the following calendar day
- Check-in/check-out or property changes
- Airport/hotel transfers and pickup instructions
- Booked excursions, tours, tickets, and meeting times
- Confirmation numbers, record locators, ticket numbers, and assigned seats
- Addresses and contact numbers when supplied
- Later e-mails that changed or canceled an earlier reservation
- Days with no booked activity (leave them open rather than inventing plans)
- Schedule conflicts or suspiciously tight connections
