# Visa and entry research checklist

Before finalizing entry requirements for a U.S. citizen, confirm that you considered:

- Every country actually entered on the itinerary
- Countries used only for international transit
- Visa / visa-free allowance and maximum stay
- eVisa, ETA, ESTA-like, or other pre-travel authorization
- Digital or paper arrival/departure forms
- Passport validity beyond the planned departure date
- Blank passport-page requirements when stated by an official source
- Proof of onward/return travel when materially required
- Special transit rules that could apply to the routing
- Whether the information came from an official government source
- The date on which the requirements were researched

Do not add health/vaccination recommendations unless the user asks for them; this skill is about immigration and entry documentation.
